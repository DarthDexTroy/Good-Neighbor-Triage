Good Neighbor Triage

Warn you before. Find you during. Pay you after.

Good Neighbor Triage turns the worst day into one text conversation. An AI agent texts people in a storm's path in their own language, ranks who needs help first during the flood, and gets claims paid fast afterward, with a scorecard that shows what it saves.

BEFORE

Prevent losses

A forecast cone shows which homes are in the path and the expected loss. Each household gets 2–4 actions picked from its own data: move the car, shutter windows, refill oxygen, send photos. They reply 1, 2 or 3 when done.

DURING

No one overlooked

Replies are scored 0–100 for urgency with a reason for every point. A silence map flags blocks where too few people answered, so the quiet ones get a door-to-door sweep.

AFTER

Fast, fair claims

Claims route to auto-pay, desk or field adjusters. Small, clean claims with pre-storm photos are paid in hours. Contractor fraud patterns are flagged for review. Flags never deny a claim.

How people use it

People don't install anything. They talk to Good Neighbor over iMessage or text, in English or Spanish.

  • We only text people who opted in (policyholders and people who message us first).
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop messages.
  • Messages never replace 911. Every danger message tells people to call 911.

About

Built in 24 hours at HackUTD 2026 for the catastrophe-response challenge. It's a concept, not an official product of any insurer. All households, phone numbers and claims in the demo are fictional. Messaging runs on Photon; the triage agent runs on NVIDIA Nemotron.
